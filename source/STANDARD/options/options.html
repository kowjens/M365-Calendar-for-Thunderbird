Microsoft data transfer

When you configure a Microsoft Entra application/tenant and choose Microsoft login, this add-on communicates with Microsoft identity services and Microsoft Graph. Calendar events, attendees, meeting metadata and invitation/contact data required by the features you request may be transmitted to Microsoft. No telemetry or user data is sent to servers operated by the add-on developer.
